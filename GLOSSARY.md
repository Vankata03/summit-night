# Glossary — Summit Night

**Route** — A named line of ascent up Everest (e.g. South Col, North Col/NE Ridge). Belongs to one of two tiers.

**Hero route** — A route given the full treatment: drawn on the terrain, climbed by an animated climber with a chase camera, and featured as a tour chapter. Currently South Col and North Col/NE Ridge.

**Secondary route** — A route drawn on the terrain without a climber or a dedicated chapter: West Ridge, Kangshung Face, Hornbein Couloir, Southwest Face.

**Spot** — A named place shown on the mountain: a waypoint on a route (camp, icefall, step, summit), a neighbouring peak, or a glacier. Not a generic "point of interest"; every spot has a name, altitude and position.

**Spotlight** — What happens when a spot is selected: the camera flies to it, a light marks it on the terrain, its label appears, and its info card opens.

**Info card** — The panel describing a spotlit spot: name, altitude, short story, routes it belongs to, and a photo with its credit.

**Tour** — The guided, linear presentation, made of chapters, which the viewer can leave at any time for free explore.

**Chapter** — One segment of the tour, e.g. the flyover, the South Col ascent, the North route.

**Free explore** — The mode where the viewer orbits the mountain and spotlights spots themselves.

**Headlamp train** — The line of climbers' headlamps moving up the South Col route during the night summit push, ending at sunrise on the summit.

**Quality tier** — One of Low, High or Cinematic; each enables a heavier set of visual effects.
